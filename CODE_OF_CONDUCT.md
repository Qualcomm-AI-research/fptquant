# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community in
all project spaces.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome differing viewpoints, give feedback
professionally, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or
other conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. Maintainers will
review reports promptly and may issue a warning, limit participation, or remove
participants from project spaces when necessary to protect the community.
